# Practical software consultancy for learning and SaaS teams

I help small education, learning-platform, and SaaS teams understand why their Rails or LMS platform is becoming slow, fragile, or difficult to extend—and turn that understanding into a practical modernization plan.

This is hands-on engineering support: investigation, clear trade-offs, and an implementation path grounded in the system you already have.

## Where I can help

### Rails and LMS modernization

- Understand a legacy Rails or learning-platform codebase
- Identify the highest-risk technical constraints
- Plan incremental modernization without an unnecessary rewrite
- Improve the path from product idea to safe delivery

### Performance and reliability

- Investigate slow requests, database queries, background jobs, and exports
- Review deployment, testing, and observability practices
- Separate symptoms from the bottlenecks that matter most
- Produce a prioritized plan rather than a list of disconnected recommendations

### Practical AI workflow readiness

- Identify useful, bounded AI opportunities in an existing product or operation
- Design privacy, approval, cost, and failure boundaries before implementation
- Decide whether a workflow needs an AI model, conventional automation, or both
- Prototype one valuable workflow instead of starting with an oversized platform

## First offer: Rails/LMS Reliability and Modernization Audit

A fixed-scope diagnostic for a team that knows its platform is holding back delivery but needs a second pair of experienced eyes.

The audit produces:

- A review of the agreed system areas and current constraints
- A concise findings report
- A prioritized risk and opportunity list
- Recommended next steps, including what *not* to change yet
- A review call to discuss the evidence and options

The scope is deliberately bounded. The first step is to understand the problem, not to sell a rewrite or promise an autonomous AI transformation.

The audit offer described on this page is still being validated with potential customers. The aim is to understand the system and produce a practical roadmap—not to sell a rewrite before the problem is understood.

## Why this work

I have more than two decades of experience building and operating web software, including Ruby on Rails systems, learning platforms, performance work, infrastructure modernization, testing, and production delivery.

My writing documents the reasoning behind the work: what was investigated, which assumptions changed, what trade-offs mattered, and how the result was verified. Confidential client and employer information is not disclosed.

## Is this a good fit?

This may be useful if:

- Your team has a working product but technical change is becoming unpredictable
- Your LMS or SaaS platform has accumulated performance or operational pain
- You need a modernization plan before committing to major implementation work
- You want practical AI advice that respects privacy, cost, and human approval
- You value a bounded investigation and clear written evidence

It is probably not a fit if you are looking for a large agency, a vague strategy presentation, or a promise to replace an entire system immediately.
